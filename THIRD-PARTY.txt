THIRD-PARTY ARTWORK

Fish artwork and color masks derive from Asciiquarium 1.1.
Copyright (C) 2003 Kirk Baucom.
Most ASCII artwork is credited upstream to Joan Stark.
Expanded marine biodiversity was backported by Claudio Matsuoka from the
Asciiquarium Android live wallpaper.
Source: https://github.com/cmatsuoka/asciiquarium
License: GNU GPL version 2 or, at your option, any later version.
See LICENSE.txt and the original notices in third-party/asciiquarium-upstream.pl.

Browser adaptations dated October 11, 2026 preserve directional drawings,
spacing, and component masks, and translate transparency markers to spaces.
The duplicate diamond design is omitted, leaving 11 unique designs.
Source for the modified browser renderer and simulation is in simulator/.
To regenerate fish-art.js, run from the package root:
  node third-party/restore-sprites.mjs

No artwork from the other researched community aquarium projects is included.

Seventeen additional creature and vehicle designs were created for ASCII Genesis: sunburst puffer,
ribbon bannerfish, silver spearfish, copper moray, violet kite ray, and
blue lanternfish, rose squid, violet octopus, sea turtle, moon jellyfish, shore crab, reef lobster, reef shark, humpback whale,
yellow submarine, lionfish, and peacock mantis shrimp. These are original project drawings, not Asciiquarium
upstream art. Their editable source is third-party/original-fish.mjs and
they are provided under GPL-2.0-or-later with this project. The combined
collection now contains 28 designs, each with both swimming directions.

The two-pose diver and its mirrored views are original ASCII Genesis art,
provided under GPL-2.0-or-later in simulator/simulator.js.

The coral, rocks, anchors, wreck debris, ridge, shipwreck, peeking eel, and distant whale scenery are original
ASCII Genesis drawings in simulator/simulator.js, GPL-2.0-or-later.

SURFACE SCENERY

The two-pose long-necked sea monster in simulator/ocean-scenes.js adapts the
original Asciiquarium drawing. Kirk Baucom and the upstream artwork credits
above apply; it is provided under GPL-2.0-or-later. The compact boat, surface
whale, water-spout frames, castle, and ruins are ASCII Genesis additions
in the same file under GPL-2.0-or-later. These scenery/visitor drawings are
separate from the regular collection of 28 creature and vehicle designs.

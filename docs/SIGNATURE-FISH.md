# Signature fish

Three original ASCII designs appear in the ordinary ocean and creature parade in Mini, Mid, and Raspberry Pi. They use the same lane and spacing rules as the other swimmers. Their names are fictional design names.

| Fish | Appearance | Entrance celebration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Balance Angelfish | Teal body, blue wing-shaped fins, pale eye | Teal and pale-green stars drift around the whole fish |
| Steadfast Marlin | Blue body, long bill, gold fins and striped detailing | Gold stars twinkle around the whole fish |
| Spark Wrasse | Orange body, violet fins, diagonal markings | Violet and orange stars orbit the whole fish |

The effect starts once the complete fish drawing is on screen, follows its body with a clear gap around all four sides, lasts three seconds, and plays once per crossing. It also appears during the parade. Particles stay away from the header, controls, and creature drawing bounds. Up to three effects can be active together. Pausing freezes the effect. Entering/leaving the parade does not restart a normal swimmer's celebration.

The collection contains **31 designs / 62 directional sprites**. Existing swimmer speeds and terrain speeds have not changed. All designs are original project artwork under GPL-2.0-or-later; the celebration code is GPL-3.0-or-later.
